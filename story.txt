2. PROJECT STORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project Title:
neostock — AI-Powered Supply Intelligence & B2B Commodity Terminal

Summary (Strictly under 150 words — 119 words):
neostock is an AI supply intelligence platform built for Tunisian grocery stores and mini-markets. Independent retailers lose 1.5% to 4% of their revenue each month to stockouts, dead capital, and opaque wholesale pricing. neostock combines Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, the Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) formula, and Tunisian agricultural seasonality to predict demand, calculate reorder points, and alert retailers days before stock runs out. It also introduces Tunisia's first live B2B wholesale terminal and collaborative group-buying pools, enabling neighborhood grocers to pool purchasing volumes and unlock direct factory discounts of up to 20%. Delivered via serverless Cloudflare Edge with 1-click WhatsApp purchase orders, neostock gives small merchants supermarket-grade supply power.

Problem Solved:
Over 120,000 independent Tunisian grocers manage inventory by guesswork, resulting in recurring stockouts on daily essentials (Milk, Coffee, Couscous), dead capital tied up in expired stock, and overpaying distributors without visibility into true wholesale market prices.

Solution and Key Features:
- Interactive Loss Simulator: Visualizes monthly cash lost to stockouts and shrinkage.
- AI Demand Forecasting & EOQ: Gemini 2.5 Flash + Wilson EOQ computes exact optimal reorder quantities and stockout alerts.
- Live B2B Wholesale Market: Tracks national commodity benchmarks (Bir El Kassâa, Sfax) and offers collective purchasing pools (-20%).
- 1-Click WhatsApp PO: Generates pre-filled wholesale purchase orders sent directly to distributors.
- Bilingual Edge App: Instant FR/EN switcher on top-left, running with sub-50ms latency on Cloudflare Edge.

Technologies Used:
React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Cloudflare Pages & Edge Functions (V8 runtime), Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, Recharts, Lucide Icons, Python FastAPI (local engine option).

Next Step:
Integrate POS barcode scanner support, mobile camera inventory reconciliation, and Darija voice-assisted stock logging.

6. TRANSPARENT AI / TOOL DISCLOSURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Models Used:
- Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (via Cloudflare Edge Functions proxy) for real-time B2B commodity analysis and RAG conversational assistance.
- Statistical Fallback Engine: Wilson EOQ formula + WMA/EMA/SMA ensemble + Tunisian retail seasonal multipliers. Guarantees 100% uptime if AI network is unavailable.

NVIDIA Brev Disclosure:
NVIDIA Brev was not used. All serverless inference and APIs run on Cloudflare Pages Edge Workers.

Datasets:
Tunisian wholesale commodity benchmarks (Bir El Kassâa, Sfax) and synthetic grocery sales histories calibrated to Tunisian retail velocity.

Security:
Zero API keys, secrets, or passwords are embedded in the client code. All API credentials are securely managed inside Cloudflare Secret Vault.
